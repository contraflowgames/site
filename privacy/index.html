Privacy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy covers the games made by Contraflow Games LLC (“we”, “us”): NeCity and NeCity Classic on Mac and, when released, iPad. The short version: our games do not collect, share or sell any information about you.

What we collect: nothing

Our games have no accounts, no sign-in of their own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and they do not send anything to us.

What the game stores on your device

To work, the game keeps some files on your own computer:

  • Your cities. Saved games and the automatic backup, including any name you give a city. They are kept in your Library’s Application Support folder, in a folder called Alpha++ (for the Mac App Store version, inside the game’s own container in ~/Library/Containers), or wherever you choose to save them. They contain only the game: the map, your city’s name, its budget and its history.
  • Your settings. Things like music volume, controls and graphics options, kept in the standard macOS preferences for the app. In the Mac App Store version this includes which add-on packs you own, so the game can start without waiting for the App Store.
  • Screenshots, only when you take one, saved where you choose.
  • Crash reports from macOS, the last few, kept only so that a bug report you choose to write can include them.

These files stay on your Mac. We cannot see them and they are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time: delete your save files, or remove the Alpha++ folder in ~/Library/Application Support/ (for the Mac App Store version, the game’s folder in ~/Library/Containers/: com.contraflowgames.necity for NeCity, com.contraflowgames.necityclassic for NeCity Classic), and the app’s preferences.

Purchases, and the store you bought from

If you got the game from the Mac App Store or Steam, Apple or Valve handle your purchase and your account under their own privacy policies. In the Mac App Store version, the game asks the App Store which add-on packs you own, and Restore Purchases asks it to bring your purchases to this Mac (Apple may ask you to sign in). The App Store tells the game only which packs you own; we do not receive your payment or account details.

If you send us a bug report

The game can write a bug-report file for you (Help → Report a Bug…). Before it writes anything, it lists what the file will hold: the game’s version, your Mac’s model, memory and macOS version, your graphics settings, the city you have open, the game’s own log, any crash reports macOS gave the game, and anything you type in the note. The log and crash reports can include file paths on your Mac, which usually contain your account name. The file stays on your Mac until you choose to send it to us. If you do, we use it only to fix the bug, and delete it when we have.

Children

Our games collect no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Our games collect no personal data. If a future version of a game starts handling any information (for example Steam achievements or opt-in crash reports), we will announce it in that version’s release notes and on this page before it takes effect, change the date above, and say what changed.
